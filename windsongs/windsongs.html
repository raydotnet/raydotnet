windsongs
link
a sequel to rainsongs that shows stages of the music creation process, using different intensities of wind as a metaphor. Each piece within the project uses a different medium to create their own ascii art "wind", the first (inner world) being through a video made using after-effects, the second (pendulum) being a webpage with rows of marquees, and the third (a step outside) being risograph cards stamped with black ink. like rainsongs, windsongs once again features original background music and corresponding visuals created in photoshop and illustrator.
